# Privacy Policy for Rattel (رتّل)

**Last updated:** [Today's Date]

Thank you for choosing **Rattel** ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect
Rattel is designed with privacy in mind. **We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII)** such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

### 1.1 Local Storage
The app uses your device's local storage (`AsyncStorage`) solely to enhance your user experience by saving:
* Your last read verse (to resume reading).
* Your display preferences (e.g., font size, theme).
* Bookmarked verses.
**This data remains on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.**

## 2. Third-Party Services and APIs
Rattel relies on trusted third-party APIs to provide Quranic content (Text, Audio, and Images). By using the app, your device requests data directly from these services:

* **AlQuran Cloud API:** Used to fetch Quranic text and metadata. [Privacy Policy](https://alquran.cloud/privacy-policy)
* **Quran.com API:** Used to fetch Quran page images (Mushaf). [Privacy Policy](https://quran.com/privacy)
* **Islamic Network (CDN):** Used to stream audio recitations.

These services may log your IP address temporarily as part of their standard server logs to prevent abuse, but "Rattel" does not have access to this data.

## 3. App Permissions
To function correctly, Rattel requires the following permissions:
* **Internet Access:** To fetch Surahs, audio, and interpretations.
* **Network State:** To check if you are connected to the internet before making requests.
* **Wake Lock:** To keep the screen or audio active during recitation playback.

## 4. Children’s Privacy
Our services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. Since we do not collect personal data from any user, this applies to children as well.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
